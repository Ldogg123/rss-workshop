Bramblewood Almanac: a reader’s guide to the spring markets

Where to find the seasonal stalls, which days are busiest, and what the weather is likely to do to the outdoor pitches.

By the Gazette newsroom · 11 March 2026

The spring markets return to the market square and the lower quay from the first weekend of April.

Saturdays remain the busiest by a wide margin. Traders on the outdoor pitches suggest arriving before ten if you want the produce stalls at their fullest.

A covered section runs along the eastern colonnade, which is where most of the stalls move to when the weather turns.
