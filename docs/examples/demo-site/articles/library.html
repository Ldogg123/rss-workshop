Central library returns to seven-day opening

Sunday hours resume for the first time since the refurbishment, alongside a restored evening timetable on weekdays.

By the Gazette newsroom · 14 March 2026

The central library will open on Sundays again from the end of this month, restoring seven-day access for the first time since the refurbishment.

Weekday evening hours also return, with the reading rooms and the local history collection staying open until eight.

The children’s library on the lower floor keeps its current timetable while work on the new entrance continues.
