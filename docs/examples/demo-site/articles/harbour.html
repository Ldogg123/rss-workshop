Harbour footbridge reopens after two-year refit

The eastern span carries pedestrians and cyclists again, with the long detour through Quay Street finally lifted.

By the Gazette newsroom · 18 March 2026

The eastern span of the harbour footbridge reopened at first light on Wednesday, ending a closure that had lasted a little over two years.

Engineers replaced the deck plates, rebuilt both approach ramps and added a separated cycle lane on the northern side. The council put the final cost at just under four million.

Morning commuters were the first across. "I have been taking the long way round through Quay Street since the spring of twenty twenty-four," said one cyclist, who arrived before the ribbon was cut.
